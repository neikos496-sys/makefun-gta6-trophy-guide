    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. Free roam stays open after the story, and there is no mission replay — keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter.

    ⚠ The one trophy to protect

    Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy the guides flag as possibly missable. Its first meditation spot is the balcony of Jason's house in Chapter 1 — after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at the house in the morning, follow her when she mentions meditating and accept. Do it before leaving Chapter 1 and keep a manual save from before it. The five stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) are also reported to have windows tied to story progress, so start each one when it first appears. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for a trophy run.
